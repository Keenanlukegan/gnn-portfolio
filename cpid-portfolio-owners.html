Automated Staff Payroll Session Counter

Shipped

Scans your source sheets and counts each staff member's actual billable sessions and customers, on the correct rate card, handling all the messy edge cases.

Why it matters

This is the revenue and session basis the whole payroll rests on, pulled from messy human-maintained grids full of merged cells, repeat customers, cancellations, substitutions, and color-coded rate types. It was reverse-engineered against the owner's confirmed-correct hand counts and encodes the exact rate rules so the machine count matches what you count by hand: replacing hours of manual tallying every pay cycle.

By the numbers
  • Two staff matched to confirmed hand counts: 41 customers / 97 sessions and 32 / 72
In one lineCounts billable sessions for payroll exactly the way you'd count them by hand: in seconds, not hours.

Automated Customer Appointment Reminders

Live

Every morning it reads the day's bookings and sends each customer a WhatsApp reminder with time, staff member, and appointment details: unattended.

Why it matters

No-shows and late arrivals are a constant drain when customers forget their appointments. This turns a manual daily chore into an unattended job, but the real engineering is defensive: messaging-web interfaces lie, so a naive sender double-sends or logs 'sent' for messages that never landed. It layers phone-first opening, three dedup guards, and post-send verification: each rule earned from a real mis-send.

By the numbers
  • 53 customers across 7 staff reminded in a single run, sending Mon–Sat
In one lineCuts no-shows by reminding every customer of their appointment each morning, with no one lifting a finger.

Inbox-to-CRM Auto-Logger

Live
